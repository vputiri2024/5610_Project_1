FLAGSHIP · IN PROGRESS

Odelia — family meal planning

The problem

Families plan meals in their heads, on the fridge, and in group texts. The result is repeated grocery runs, food that goes to waste, and the same six dinners on rotation. My background in nutrition and in running a restaurant kitchen is where this product starts.

Product decisions

The first version plans a week for one household and builds the shopping list from it. Recipe discovery, sharing, and nutrition tracking were cut from v1 so the core loop could ship and be tested with a real family.

How it is built

A web application with a small relational data model: households, members, meals, and a weekly plan. The stack and the tradeoffs still open are documented alongside the code.

AI in the product

An assistant suggests a week of meals from what the household already likes and what is in the pantry. The plan itself, the list, and the schedule stay deterministic so the family can trust them.

How I build

AI is in the loop. I decide where the line sits.

I build AI products and I build with AI. The skill is knowing what to delegate, what to own, and how to check what comes back.

I delegate

Scaffolding, boilerplate, first-draft components, test generation, and documentation lookups. Tasks where speed matters more than authorship.

I own

Scope, the data model, architecture, product decisions, review of every line that ships, and the call on what not to build.

I verify

Tests, reading the diff, running it, and recognizing when a model is confidently wrong. Nothing is merged on trust.
