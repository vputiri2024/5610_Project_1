Project Description
I have very diverse backgrounds in both education and work. I have gone to culinary school, music school, studied finance, entrepreneurship and now computer science. I have worked in project management, product development in financial services and have I started two successful companies which have both been in existence for over a decade. While I have a great number of accomplishments, the challenge hiring managers have when they look at my resume is that it does not demonstrate a clear path most job candidates take for positions for which they apply.
For project one, I decided on a site that introduces me in a way that ties these diverse paths together into a clearer picture of my interests and strengths to better describe my career. I want to position myself as a Full-Stack Product Developer. This is not simply a Full-Stack Engineer or Developer. While that is part of it, I also want to highlight my experience in product research, delivery and entrepreneurship. The goal is to show how my collective demonstrated experiences and credentials form the scaffolding of a value creation pipeline.

User Personas

Me:
The main user of this site will be me. The goal which is mentioned above is to have a way to introduce my skillsets prior to getting an interview. I think of it as an additional way to market myself and tell my story in a way that resumes and LinkedIn cannot. I also want this to be a jumping off point for future technology projects that demonstrate what I have done.

Hiring Manager:
Resumes do not always paint a great picture of a person, and you are trying to glean a lot of it from a small amount of dense information in a resume. Trying to make important decisions about who to move forward with in the candidate pool is a challenge.

User Stories

Me:
As a user I would like a website that concisely describes my career in a way that makes it easy to understand and confers that value that I bring in order to be differentiated and more marketable and can compete for the best jobs.

Hiring Manager:
As a hiring manager who reviews many applicants for relevant skills, I would like an easy way to gage an applicant’s skillset and assess the value and skills he or she brings to a role.
